ABOUT ✦

Meet Rathi

Rathi Bridal Artist is a beauty parlour run by Rathi, who has spent 7 years creating bridal, party and everyday looks. Our services cover basic and advanced hair cuts, HD, airbrush and bridal make-up, saree draping for every occasion, and beautician courses. Every bride is treated with care from the first consultation to the final touch, so she feels confident on her big day. You can book your appointment online or visit our studio in Periyakulam. We also train aspiring beauticians in hair care, make-up artistry and salon hygiene, so you can build a career in beauty.
